# Business Questions

Every question below is answered by at least one SQL query, Python analysis or Power BI visual.

## 01 Growth Overview

1. What are MRR, ARR, paying customers and ARPA at each month end?
2. How fast is MRR growing month over month?
3. How much MRR comes from new customers, expansion, contraction and churn each month (the MRR bridge)?
4. What are net revenue retention (NRR) and gross revenue retention (GRR)?
5. Which plan (Starter, Pro, Business) contributes the most MRR?
6. Which countries contribute the most MRR?

## 02 Acquisition & Conversion

1. How many users reach each funnel step: signup, onboarding, activation, trial, paid?
2. Where is the biggest drop-off?
3. What is the signup-to-paid conversion rate by acquisition channel?
4. How many days does it take a user to go from signup to paid?
5. What share of users activate (create a project) within 7 days?
6. Does conversion differ by company size or device?

## 03 Cohort Retention Analysis

1. What share of each monthly cohort is still active after 1, 3, 6 and 12 months?
2. Are newer cohorts retaining better or worse than older ones?
3. How does retention differ by channel, plan and billing cycle?
4. How long does a typical customer stay (survival curve and median lifetime)?

## 04 Churn, CAC & Customer Value

1. What is the monthly logo churn rate and revenue churn rate?
2. Is churn concentrated in the first months of a customer's life?
3. Which plans, channels and billing cycles churn the most?
4. How much churn is voluntary and how much is involuntary (failed payments)?
5. What are the CAC, LTV, LTV:CAC and CAC payback period for each channel?
6. Which customer segments hold the most revenue, and which are at risk?
7. Which customers are most likely to churn next month?
